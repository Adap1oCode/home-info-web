How an order actually runs

Four steps — and we do the chasing in all of them.

1

You send it over

Portal, email, or a phone call if that is quicker. An address and a title number is usually enough for us to work out the rest.

2

We chase, not you

We deal with the councils directly and follow up on anything outstanding. If a search is going to be late, you should hear it from us first.

3

A person reads it

Read against the property, the title and the enquiry, so that what comes back answers the question you actually asked.

4

Delivered, with a note

Straight to your case file. If there’s something you’ll want to look at twice, we say so at the top rather than leaving you to find it.

Where the data comes from

Direct integrations with the organisations that hold the data.

Our systems connect directly to the suppliers and registries the reports come from, so what reaches your file is what the source returned.

Environmental data through Landmark, drainage and water through Martello, official copies and local land charges direct from HM Land Registry, licensed Ordnance Survey mapping, and ID, AML, company and lawyer checks through Veriphy.

We are registered with the PCCB as a Search Code subscriber, and a member of IPSA and COPSO.
